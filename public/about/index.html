I’m Sabeer, an entrepreneur based in Abu Dhabi. My perspective on software comes from working with people, customers, operations, and the decisions a business makes every day.

Through Top To Bottom Cleaning Services, where Donna serves as Operations Manager, I have worked with bookings, staff coordination, drivers, service quality, administration, and business communication. Those experiences give my product ideas a practical starting point.

Vital Management Consultancy is a UAE business consultancy in which Sabeer and Donna work together as business partners, bringing practical experience in business setup, licensing, government services, and management support.

My role is to bring requirements, domain knowledge, decisions, and persistent review. AI tools and technical collaborators help turn those ideas into implementations that still need thoughtful testing and validation.

Most of this work has grown through an ongoing collaboration with ChatGPT and Codex, with other software and AI tools contributing to parts of the journey.
